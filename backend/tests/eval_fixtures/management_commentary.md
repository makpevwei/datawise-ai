## Overview

Sales performance in Q1 2024 was mixed across product categories and
shipping methods. This note summarizes management's read on the quarter
ahead of the board review.

## Regional Performance

Management believes the West Africa region underperformed relative to
other regions this quarter, and attributes the softness primarily to
slower fulfillment through Standard Class shipping.

## Product Category Commentary

The Footwear category remained the strongest contributor to order volume.
Golf category orders were comparatively small but management does not
consider this a concern given the category's niche positioning.

## Payment Trends

Management notes a continued customer preference for credit-based payment
methods over cash, which it attributes to the growth of online ordering.

## Risks Flagged by Management

Management flagged late-delivery risk as an ongoing operational concern,
particularly for Standard Class and Second Class shipping modes, and
recommended further investigation into carrier performance in that area.
